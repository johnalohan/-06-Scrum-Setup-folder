## Scrum Master Reflection
### Obstacle and Response
[One impediment that could affect the progress towards the sprint goal is an imbalanced task distribution. 
Because the Scheduling WorkFlow Prototype is huge, it is likely that only a few developers have the skillset requires 
to build the workflow. If one of those developers falls ill or simply can't perform the job then we will be looking at 
some serious delays. We would have to increase the budget to outsource more help. The Scrum Master can make the experienced
developer team up with a non experienced developer to work on the Scheduling Workflow Prototype. That way the experienced
developer can teach the non experienced developer incase he goes absent. ]
